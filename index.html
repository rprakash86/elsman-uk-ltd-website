GOOD TO KNOW

Questions,
answered.

Some useful information for retailers considering an enquiry.

Who can enquire about wholesale products?+

Our catalogue is intended for convenience stores, independent shops and other retail businesses. Please include your business name and location when contacting us.

Do you display wholesale prices online?+

Prices are not displayed in this catalogue. Contact us with the products and quantities you're interested in so that we can discuss current wholesale pricing.

Can I enquire about bulk quantities?+

Yes. Include the product names and approximate quantities in your message. Our team can discuss availability and the next steps with you.

Are all products always in stock?+

Product availability can change. Please confirm current stock, specifications and lead times with us before placing an order.

Can I ask about a product not shown here?+

Of course. Send us a message describing what you're looking for and we'll let you know if we can help.
